## 3. Mental Model Application

**What would make DECK fail?**

1. **UGG Falls Out of Fashion Permanently**
   - Probability: 15%
   - Impact: -40% revenue
   - Mitigation: HOKA growth, international expansion

2. **Nike/Adidas Crushes HOKA**
   - Probability: 20%
   - Impact: -30% growth
   - Mitigation: HOKA has tech lead (stacked midsole), cult following

3. **China Relationship Deteriorates**
   - Probability: 25%
   - Impact: -18% revenue
   - Mitigation: Geographic diversification

4. **Management Makes Big Acquisition Mistake**
   - Probability: 10%
   - Impact: -20% value
   - Mitigation: Track record is disciplined

5. **Recession Crushes Discretionary Spending**
   - Probability: 30% (within 5 years)
   - Impact: -20% earnings temporarily
   - Mitigation: Strong balance sheet survives, recovers

**Probability-Weighted Failure Risk:** 20%

### Pre-Mortem Analysis

**"We lost money on DECK because..."**

1. **"We underestimated UGG fashion risk"**  
   Gen Z truly moved on, brand lost relevance, inventory write-downs crushed margins.

2. **"We overpaid at $83 thinking it was cheap"**  
   Multiple compression from 20x to 12x earnings as growth slowed.

3. **"We missed Nike's competitive response"**  
   Nike launched competing comfort tech and crushed HOKA's advantage.

4. **"China got worse, not better"**  
   Geopolitical issues eliminated China as growth driver permanently.

**Probability of Permanent Capital Loss:** LOW (15%)
*Key: Even if UGG fades, HOKA and balance sheet provide floor.*
