# Buffett/Munger Investment Analysis Framework

## Instructions for Dexter

When analyzing any company, apply BOTH modes:
1. **Quantitative** - Financial metrics (SRC-compatible)
2. **Qualitative** - Business quality (Buffett/Munger style)

**Key Principle:** Business-first, not spreadsheet-first. A great business at a fair price > fair business at a great price.

### 3. Management Quality

**Buffett's Three Questions:**

#### A. Is management rational?
- Capital allocation decisions history
- Response to industry changes
- Strategic clarity and consistency
- **Rating:** A/B/C/D/F

#### B. Is management candid with shareholders?
- Acknowledges mistakes in reports
- Clear communication of challenges
- Doesn't sugarcoat bad news
- **Rating:** A/B/C/D/F

#### C. Does management resist institutional imperative?
- Doesn't copy competitors' bad decisions
- Focuses on long-term vs. quarterly earnings
- Doesn't dilute shareholders unnecessarily
- **Rating:** A/B/C/D/F

## Mode 3: Mental Model Application (Charlie Munger)

### Inversion Analysis

**Instead of asking "Why will this succeed?" ask "What would make this fail?"**

#### Failure Scenarios
1. **Economic Downturn:** Revenue drops %, can they survive?
2. **Competitive Attack:** New entrant with lower prices/better tech
3. **Regulatory Change:** New laws that hurt business model
4. **Key Person Loss:** Impact of losing CEO/key executives
5. **Technology Disruption:** New tech makes product obsolete
6. **Customer Concentration:** Loss of top 3 customers = % revenue hit

**Probability-Weighted Failure Risk:** %
