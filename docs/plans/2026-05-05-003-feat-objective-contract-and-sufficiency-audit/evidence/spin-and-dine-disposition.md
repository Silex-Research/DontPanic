## Recommendations for sufficiency prompt / schema revisions

PASS verdict, so no required revisions. Optional polish for future evolution:

- **Prompt tuning:** the auditor correctly identified the Pro-tier voice/family gap as a wiring gap, but its recommendation suggests "either add features OR document in non_goals". That's good operator-facing nuance. Future prompt iteration could explicitly instruct the auditor to consider non_goals as a valid mitigation, reducing the chance of a future operator dispositioning it as noise.
- **Schema:** ObjectiveContract.user_journeys[*].states is optional (no `minItems`). For parity goals specifically, requiring states helps the auditor anchor parity-gap findings on enumerated state coverage. Consider a future v1.4.x or v1.5.0 bump that makes `states` recommended (not required) for parity goal_type.
